# FadFada Gallery Screenshot Selection

Source folder: `scripts/demo-output/Screens`

Use these 15 screenshots for the submission gallery. They show the strongest product story: core AI chat, Gemini-powered workflows, parent/child separation, child-safe learning, and operational trust.

## 1. F_1.png - Main AI Companion Chat

FadFada opens as a calm mobile-first AI companion with chat, voice, persona controls, breathing tools, and quick actions in one focused screen. This introduces the core user experience and shows that the product is more than a plain chatbot.

## 2. F_2.png - Companion Persona Picker

The persona drawer shows specialized companions for planning, creativity, emotional support, business, learning, and guidance. Each companion changes the Gemini prompt context so users can receive a response with the right role and tone.

## 3. F_4.png - Daily Pulse Check-In

Daily Pulse lets users choose mood, energy, and what they need before starting a guided reflection. These structured inputs become AI context, helping Gemini produce a more relevant and supportive next step.

## 4. F_6.png - Smart Continuity And Progress

FadFada remembers meaningful threads and lets users continue a previous topic, save a snapshot, or turn progress into a small quest. This shows continuity, memory-like product behavior, and repeated-use value.

## 5. F_7.png - Moment Actions

Useful AI replies can become saved moments, tiny plans, proof cards, downloadable capsules, shared replies, or softer follow-ups. This shows how FadFada turns Gemini responses into reusable product artifacts.

## 6. F_8.png - Parent Fast Access Tools

The parent workspace includes homework transformer, homework follow-up, parent playbook, and child plans. This screenshot introduces the family product layer and shows that parent tools are separated from the child experience.

## 7. F_9.png - Homework Transformer Upload Flow

Parents can choose a child, upload a worksheet image or use the camera, select the child stage, add a note, and send the activity to the child workspace. This is the key entry point for Gemini multimodal homework understanding.

## 8. F_10.png - Child Profiles And Separation

The profile screen explains separate child spaces under one parent account, with separate histories and no child password management. This supports the safety story around identity separation and parent-controlled access.

## 9. F_12.png - Gemini Homework Transformation Result

The uploaded worksheet is transformed into a child-ready activity with detected subject, task description, child intro, and guided steps. This is the strongest proof of Gemini multimodal input plus structured JSON output becoming an interactive product workflow.

## 10. F_13.png - Child Workspace Home

The app switches into child mode for Rana, greets the child by name, shows child-safe companions, and removes adult tools. This screenshot proves role-aware UI and child identity isolation.

## 11. F_14.png - Interactive Child Homework Activity

The child sees a picture challenge with a question, voice controls, and answer choices. This shows how a static worksheet becomes a child-safe learning game with guided interaction.

## 12. F_15.png - Story Journey Passport

The child workspace includes a story passport with reading progress, minutes, next adventure, and an adventure trail. This shows retention, learning continuity, and a safe child engagement loop.

## 13. F_17.png - Interactive Story Reader

Stories can be read page by page, played with auto-read, turned into a quiz, or launched into a guided session. This shows how story content becomes an interactive learning and reflection surface.

## 14. F_19.png - Child Companion Picker

Child mode has its own companion roster, including child-friendly personas for riddles, focus, manners, space, logic, nature, creativity, and stories. This reinforces that adult personas are not available in the child experience.

## 15. F_20.png - Parent Pulse Snapshot And Return Code

The parent profile shows return-code control, child activity history, risk level, recent trends, and a suggested family ritual. This closes the gallery with parent oversight, safety, and role separation.
